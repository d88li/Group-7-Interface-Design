Frequently Asked Questions

The subsidy scheme is set up to encourage students of PolyU School of Design (SD) to participate in international competitions and awards which are helpful to enhance their global exposure and add value to their portfolios.

Eligible students may claim reimbursement for official competition entrance/registration fees and artwork or prototype shipping allowances, up to an annual maximum subsidy of HK$9,066 per student.

Applications are accepted year-round within the current academic cycle. Applicants must notify the SD Marketing Team of competition results within one month of official announcement, and approved reimbursements are disbursed within 14 business days after receipt verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Full-time undergraduate (BA in Design) and taught postgraduate students across PolyU School of Design specialisms—including Interaction Design, Advertising Design, Communication Design, Environmental and Interior Design, Fashion and Textile Design, Industrial and Product Design, Media Design, and Social Design—are eligible.

Applicants must be either (a) a current student or (b) a recent graduate* of one of the eligible programmes/specialisms.

* Recent graduates are defined as those who graduated within one year of the application date, i.e. Class of 2025, and are only eligible if submitting their school projects to recognized competitions.

Applicants must obtain endorsement from their Programme Leader or a designated delegate, or Subject Leaders for entries resulted from interdisciplinary work, before submitting the application. In-bound exchange students are not eligible.

Subsidies cover internationally or regionally recognized design awards and juried student competitions aligned with School of Design disciplines. Competitions without an independent jury or commercial pay-to-win directories are excluded.

All funded submissions must credit “School of Design, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University” as the affiliated institution on competition entry forms and published credits.

Before submitting, applicants must list their Programme Leader, designated delegate, or Subject Leader (for interdisciplinary coursework) as Academic Endorser. Upon submission, the endorser receives a verification notification to approve the project’s quality and eligibility.

Entries funded by other PolyU or external financial schemes, late applications submitted without prior endorsement, and claims lacking official payment receipts are not eligible for reimbursement.
